      What is listed

      Meetings run by a scholarly or professional society, in the US or internationally, or funded by a US federal agency such as NSF. Meetings run by for-profit conference companies, and any meeting that might be predatory, are left out; if we are not sure, it is not listed. Each file is checked against the sponsor's own pages on the date shown.

      Three types are listed: education conferences; discipline meetings with education sessions, divisions, or tracks; and meetings in History & Philosophy of Science, Human Dimensions, and Science & Health Communication, which cover those fields themselves as well as teaching in them. Disciplines use the same categories as STEMEd+ Jobs, Funding, the STEMEd+ Digest, and the STEM+ Education Research Graduate Programs list. Meetings open to all STEM+ fields appear under every discipline.

      Dates marked "confirm dates" were announced by the sponsor or a partner society but are not yet on the meeting's own page.
